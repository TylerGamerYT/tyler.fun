Moved to Vercel
& profiles restructured

A major behind-the-scenes update that moved tyler.fun to Vercel and gave user profiles a cleaner, more consistent URL structure.

April 2026

✦ New

  • Moved tyler.fun to Vercel.
  • Restructured user profiles into the new /users/ path.
  • Added a cleaner foundation for managing profile pages and future account-related features.

◎ Profile Structure

  • User profiles were moved into a dedicated /users/ section.
  • Profile URLs became more organized and consistent.
  • The new structure made it easier to keep user pages separate from the rest of the site's routes.
/users/username

↗ Infrastructure

  • Vercel became the new hosting and deployment platform for tyler.fun.
  • The site's routing structure was prepared for cleaner URLs and additional pages.
  • The move provided a stronger foundation for the site's continued development.

★ Why this update?

tyler.fun needed a better foundation.

Moving to Vercel and restructuring profiles made the site easier to deploy, organize and expand. This update also set up the routing structure that would become even cleaner in v1.1.
