v1.5

French & Spanish
Language Support

tyler.fun can now be used in French and Spanish, with a new language selector in Settings. More languages are planned for the future.

October 2026

✦ New

  • Added French language support.
  • Added Spanish language support.
  • Added a language selector to Settings. Your choice is saved on your device and the page reloads to apply it.
  • Added a notice above the language selector explaining that translations may be incomplete or inaccurate, and that changing the language reloads the page.

↗ Changed

  • Translations currently cover the home page, Settings, log in and sign up pages. Missing translations fall back to English.
  • Added a View More button to News & Releases. Releases older than v1.0 are now hidden until you click it.

◇ Coming Soon

  • Arabic — planned, 0% complete. Not available yet.
  • Polish — planned, 0% complete. Not available yet.

◎ Why this update?

tyler.fun is for everyone.

Following the Settings panel introduced in v1.4, v1.5 adds the first extra languages to tyler.fun. Translations are still a work in progress and may be missing or inaccurate on some pages, and Arabic and Polish are listed as coming soon while they are planned.
