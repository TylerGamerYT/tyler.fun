v0.8

Profiles,
search & accounts

A major step toward making tyler.fun feel like a connected platform, introducing profiles, system accounts, search and a better guest experience.

April 2026

✦ New

  • Added user profiles.
  • Added system accounts for important site identities.
  • Added a search bar to help find things across the site.
  • Added guest mode so visitors can use the site without needing an account.

◎ Profiles

  • Users could now have dedicated profile pages.
  • Profiles introduced a central place for account information and identity.
  • System accounts established a way for official site identities to exist alongside regular users.
User Profiles
Dedicated pages for users and their account identity.
System Accounts
Special accounts for official site-related identities.

⌕ Search & Guest Mode

  • Added a site-wide search bar.
  • Made it easier to discover pages and users without manually navigating through the site.
  • Added guest mode for visitors who wanted to browse without signing in.

★ Why this update?

tyler.fun was becoming more than a collection of games.

v0.8 introduced the first real foundation for users, profiles and site-wide discovery. With search, system accounts and guest mode, the site became easier to explore while preparing for the account features that would arrive in later releases.
