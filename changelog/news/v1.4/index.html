v1.4

Custom roles,
profile themes &
activity feeds

Building on our account identity system with personalized user profiles, community role integration, and real-time activity timelines.

September 2026

✦ New

  • Added custom roles & titles to highlight community contributors and special account designations.
  • Added profile theme customization allowing users to choose custom color accents and banners.
  • Added live activity feeds to showcase profile updates, earned badges, and recent interactions.
  • Expanded profile privacy settings with granular control over who can view your activity feed.

↗ Changed

  • Redesigned profile headers to seamlessly integrate custom banners with existing badge collections.
  • Optimized background canvas performance for smoother rendering across mobile browsers.
  • Streamlined account settings for faster navigation between profile details and verification controls.

◎ Why this update?

Profiles are now personal spaces.

Following the foundational account identity updates in v1.3, v1.4 gives users the tools to personalize their space and share their activity across tyler.fun. Custom roles and themes allow community members to express themselves while keeping navigation clean and performant.
